The metrics, in plain terms

Everything is per 100 possessions, so fast and slow teams compare fairly. League average this season is about 108 points per 100.

AdjOAdjusted offensive efficiency: points a team would score per 100 possessions against an average defense, with home court taken out. Higher is better.
AdjDAdjusted defensive efficiency: points allowed per 100 possessions against an average offense, venue removed. Lower is better.
AdjEMAdjusted efficiency margin: AdjO minus AdjD. Read it as "how many points better than an average team, per 100 possessions." The headline rating; the table is ranked by it.
BarthagThe chance of beating an average Division I team on a neutral floor, turned into a number between 0 and 1 from AdjEM. .900 means a 90% favorite in that game.
PacePossessions per 40 minutes. Style, not quality: fast teams simply get more trips.
Game AdjO / AdjDThe same adjustment applied to one game: what the offense/defense did that night, corrected for who the opponent was and where it was played. That is what the chart and the game log show.
PriorEarly in the season, last season's final rating is blended in and fades out by game 27, so November ratings are steadier than a handful of games deserve.
